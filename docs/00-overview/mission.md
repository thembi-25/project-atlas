# Mission

## Mission statement

Give every plumbing, HVAC, and electrical business the operational backbone of a much larger company — accurate scheduling, a trustworthy customer and property record, fast estimates and payments — without forcing them to become software experts or stitch together five disconnected tools.

## What the mission means in practice

1. **Replace the spreadsheet-and-phone-call stack.** Most small field-service businesses (2–50 technicians) run on a patchwork of a basic scheduling tool, paper or PDF invoices, text messages for dispatch, and a shoebox of warranty cards. Atlas replaces that patchwork with one system covering the full job lifecycle: intake → scheduling → dispatch → on-site work → estimate → invoice → payment. See [Core Operations modules](../06-modules/jobs-prd.md).
2. **Make the property, not the job, the durable record.** Every job attaches to a property and, where applicable, to specific assets inside it. A technician arriving at a property for the first time can still see what equipment is installed, when it was last serviced, and what work is outstanding — because the record was built by every technician before them. See [Properties](../03-domain/properties.md) and [Assets](../03-domain/assets.md).
3. **Remove the back-office tax.** Owners of small field-service businesses spend disproportionate time on invoicing, chasing payment, and reconciling parts costs. Atlas automates the estimate-to-invoice-to-payment chain and keeps inventory and job costing connected, so the numbers are correct without manual re-entry. See [Invoicing PRD](../06-modules/invoicing-prd.md) and [Payments PRD](../06-modules/payments-prd.md).
4. **Build the platform other participants want to connect to.** Suppliers want fewer, better-qualified orders. Manufacturers want verified installations and warranty compliance. Lenders want trustworthy job and payment histories. None of that is possible without pillar 1 (Core Operations) being genuinely reliable first — trust is earned operationally before it is monetized structurally.

## Who benefits, and how

- **Business owners** get visibility into revenue, technician utilization, and job profitability without hiring an analyst. See [Target Customers](./target-customers.md).
- **Dispatchers/office managers** get a single scheduling and dispatch board instead of a whiteboard and a phone. See [Scheduling PRD](../06-modules/scheduling-prd.md), [Dispatch PRD](../06-modules/dispatch-prd.md).
- **Technicians** get the property and asset history on their phone before they knock on the door. See [Mobile PRD](../06-modules/mobile-prd.md).
- **Customers** get transparent estimates, digital invoices, and a self-service portal instead of chasing a callback. See [Customer Portal PRD](../06-modules/customer-portal-prd.md).

## Non-mission

Atlas's mission is not to maximize technician headcount reduction through automation, nor to insert AI into every workflow. If a workflow is better served by a human decision, we build tools that inform that decision, not tools that make it invisibly. See [Product Principles](./product-principles.md).
