## Goal

The quote-to-cash flow that closes the loop opened by Sprint 4's Jobs: a Job can now be quoted (Estimate), the quote approved by the Customer, the completed work billed (Invoice), and the Invoice paid (Payment) — either by staff recording cash/check, or by the Customer paying online through a new, minimal Customer Portal surface. This sprint introduces two things this codebase has not needed before: real money handling backed by a live payment processor (Stripe, per [ADR-018](../11-adr/ADR-018-payments.md)), and a second, non-staff authentication surface (the Customer Portal's Contact-facing magic-link login). Both are scoped deliberately narrowly — see "Scope decisions" below.

### Scope decisions

- **Stripe integration**: `packages/integrations/stripe` is implemented as full, structurally-correct integration code per ADR-018 — PaymentIntent creation for online/Portal card payments, webhook signature verification and handling, idempotency keys on every capture. This sandbox has no configured Stripe credentials, so the integration cannot be exercised against Stripe's live API; it is written and ready for real test-mode keys, mirroring how prior sprints' database integration tests were written but could not be executed in this sandbox. Stripe Terminal (card-present payment, mobile SDK/hardware) is explicitly deferred — out of scope for this sprint, mirroring Sprint 4's deferral of a dedicated mobile app package.
- **Customer Portal**: this sprint builds only the minimal Contact-facing magic-link auth flow (reusing the `portal_access_enabled` flag on `crm.contacts` from Sprint 2) plus the two PRD-mandated transactional views — Estimate approve/reject and Invoice payment. It does not build the full [Customer Portal PRD](../06-modules/customer-portal-prd.md) feature set (Properties/Jobs browsing, PDF download, Documents, etc.); those remain out of scope unless a later sprint's exit criteria require them.

## Deliverables

1. `financials` Postgres schema: `estimates`, `estimate_line_items`, `invoices`, `invoice_line_items`, `credit_notes`, `payments`, plus per-Organization sequential-number counters for Estimates and Invoices — with RLS enabled/forced, following the established Sprint 1–4 pattern. Per [Estimates](../03-domain/estimates.md), [Invoices](../03-domain/invoices.md), [Payments](../03-domain/payments.md), [Schema Overview](../04-database/schema-overview.md).
2. A `platform.domain_events` transactional-outbox table and a Worker-side dispatcher, per [ADR-011](../11-adr/ADR-011-event-history.md) and [ADR-016](../11-adr/ADR-016-background-jobs.md) — the first genuine use of `apps/worker`'s already-installed pg-boss dependency. `job.completed` gets a real consumer: when a completed Job has an `approved` Estimate, the Worker auto-generates a **draft** Invoice mirroring that Estimate's line items. `estimate.approved`, `invoice.finalized`, and `payment.received` are written to the outbox per the documented event catalog but have no active consumer yet (no Notifications/Analytics module exists), matching the established deferral pattern from prior sprints. `job.dispatched` is not implemented this sprint — no consumer exists for it yet and Sprint 4's scheduling code is left untouched.
3. Estimate CRUD + explicit transition-command endpoints (`/send`, `/approve`, `/reject`, `/convert`) and Invoice CRUD + transition-command endpoints (`/finalize`, `/send`, `/void`) rather than an unrestricted `status` `PATCH`, per [Resource Conventions](../05-api/resource-conventions.md), matching Sprint 4's endpoint-shape precedent. Payment capture (`POST /api/v1/invoices/{id}/payments`) for staff-recorded cash/check and Stripe-backed card payments, plus `POST /api/v1/webhooks/stripe` for asynchronous payment confirmation per [Webhooks](../05-api/webhooks.md).
4. `packages/integrations/stripe` — a typed wrapper around the Stripe SDK per ADR-018: PaymentIntent creation, refunds, and webhook signature verification, reading `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`/`STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` from environment configuration.
5. Customer Portal auth: Contact-facing magic-link generation and redemption (mirroring Sprint 1's Membership-invitation-token pattern), linking a Contact to an `identity.users` row via a new `portal_user_id` column, with portal session resolution kept distinct from staff `getAuthenticatedUser()`. Portal endpoints live under a distinct `/api/v1/portal/*` path, per the Customer Portal PRD's explicit routing-layer scoping requirement. RLS for portal access is Contact-scoped (`contacts.portal_user_id = auth.uid()`), distinct from Membership-based staff policies.
6. Staff UI: Estimate and Invoice list/detail views with a line-item builder, state-transition actions, and payment recording. Portal UI: magic-link login, an Estimate approve/reject view, and an Invoice payment view — per both PRDs' UI Requirements, scoped per "Scope decisions" above.
7. Full test coverage: unit (Estimate/Invoice/Payment state machines, money math, idempotency-key logic), integration (RLS/tenant isolation including Contact-portal cross-tenant scenarios, the ten mandatory tenant-isolation scenarios, lifecycle, permission matrix), API (CRUD + transition endpoints + permission matrix + portal endpoints).

## Exit criteria

A Job can be quoted with an Estimate, the Estimate sent and approved or rejected by the Customer (staff-recorded or via the Portal), an approved Estimate converted to a finalized Invoice (manually or auto-generated on Job completion), and the Invoice paid — by staff recording cash/check or by the Customer paying by card through the Portal, with card payments confirmed only via the Stripe webhook, never the synchronous client response — with every transition validated against the documented state machines, `amount_paid`/`balance_due` always computed from linked non-refunded Payments, financial immutability preserved (no editing a finalized Invoice's line items/totals; corrections only via Credit Note or Void), and full tenant isolation verified end to end, including that Organization A can never read, modify, approve, or pay Organization B's Estimates/Invoices, and a Contact can never access another Customer's Estimates/Invoices through the Portal.
